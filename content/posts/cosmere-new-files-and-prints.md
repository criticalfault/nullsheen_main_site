I've been doing a lot of prep work lately (All for Gamehole Con) and in the process I ended up making a pile of new Cosmere files that are worth sharing. This is a collection of Tokens, trackers and reference cards. Rather than just quietly add them to the [Cosmere page](/cosmere/) and hope you notice, I figured a proper post was in order.

Here's everything new that's up now.

---

## 3D Prints

This is the bulk of what's new. If you've been running the Cosmere RPG at the table, these will give your players a big smile when you toss them various trinkets.

### Stormlight Tokens

A token built for tracking Investiture for the Knights Radiance. It's the simplified symbol for the Stormlight Archive, the prints came out very clean. Will show some pictures of them in my actual Gamehole Con 2026 post.

[Download the Knights Radiant Token (.3mf)](/files/Cosmere/KnightsRadiant_Token.3mf)

### Double-Sided Turn Tracker & Reaction Coin

This one is genuinely clever. I found this on the Bambu Lab models search, but it has slow turns on one side, fast turn on the other with a coin for reaction built into the side of it. You can flip each turn and easily track players action orders and reactions. This is one I'll be using at every session going forward. This one does REQUIRE an AMS to work. Otherwise you could print and paint if thats your thing.

[Download the Double-Sided Turn Tracker & Reaction Coin (.3mf)](/files/Cosmere/Doublesided_Turn_Tracker_and_Reaction_Coin.3mf)

### Ettmetal Token

A token built for tracking Investiture for the characters in Mistborn. It's the symbol for Harmonium, which appears on the fancy dice box set for Mistborn, so I thought it fitting.

*(Two versions are available on the [Cosmere page](/cosmere/) — grab whichever fits your printer setup best.)*

## Reference Documents

These were already living on the Cosmere page but deserve a spotlight here since not everyone checks that page regularly.

### Radiant Reference Cards

[Download the Radiant Reference Cards (PDF)](/files/Cosmere/RadiantReferenceCards.pdf)

Quick-reference cards for the Radiant orders. Print them out and hand them to your players at session zero so nobody has to flip through the Handbook mid-scene trying to remember what Gravitation does. Or a great way to help players who swear ideals.

### Cartas de Referencia de Radiantes

[Download the Radiant Reference Cards in Spanish (PDF)](/files/Cosmere/CartasReferenciaRadiantes.pdf)

Same thing, Spanish version. If you've got players or run your games in Spanish, this one's for you.

### Radiant Vows

[Download the Radiant Vows (SVG)](/files/Cosmere/RadiantVows.svg)

A printable SVG of the first Radiant Oath. Good for a prop handout or just for printing and putting on your wall.
