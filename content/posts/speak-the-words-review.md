## How I got here

Its not often that I get the chance to run something very few people have ever had their hands on. Now I know it's been fully released and more people have had their chance to take their spin on it, but when I was able to get my hands on it, it was ahead of the crowd. Cosmere is something I have only recently got into. A friend of mine raved about it to me and I was deeply intimidated by it. Just 20 something novels, most of which were 800+ pages each to get through to get the lay of the land. That seems completely insurmountable until I was introduced to the Graphic Audio versions of the stories. This was a game changer and ultimately, i devoured all of Stormlight Archive, Mistborn, etc in a matter of months.  I enjoyed it so much that I even decided to sign up as an affiliate, to try to get more people introduced to this wonderful world.

All of this being said, I eventually discovered that Brotherwise Games had gotten the Cosmere TTRPG license and was running the games for Stormlight Archive and now Mistborn. This of course peaked my interest as a near obessessive reader and game designer/writer/running myself. So I ran into this as quickly as I could. They run something they call the Envoy program for those who want to teach their games to others. I tried a few sessions before I started running a campaign of my own then signed up. I travel the midwest to go to various conventions to run all kinds of games, whatever I am passionate about. Next year will be the big year of running Stormlight Archive and some Mistborn (Once I get a chance to get my hands on the books and am able to read through them of course). All of this is to say, I want to run a story for new people and find a good niche for myself to introduce people into the amazing heroics of Stormlight Archive.
