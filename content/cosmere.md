### What Each Book Is

**Stormlight Handbook** — The core rulebook. Everything you need to build characters and run games in the Stormlight setting. 392 pages covering the Plotweaver system, character creation, Surgebinding, and all core mechanics.

**Stormlight World Guide** — Deep lore on Roshar: geography, factions, history, and adversary profiles. An essential companion to the Handbook.

**Stonewalkers** — A full adventure created in partnership with Brandon Sanderson and Dan Wells, revealing new secrets of the Stormlight setting. Requires the Stormlight Handbook.

**Welcome to Roshar** — A spoiler-free setting guide that introduces new players to the world of Roshar. Great handout for players who haven't read the books yet.

**Bridge Nine** — An introductory 1st-level adventure set on the Shattered Plains. Great entry point for new GMs and players. Designed to teach the game's mechanics while you play.

**The First Step** — A "session zero" adventure where players start with blank character sheets. Their choices during the adventure determine the characters they create — and the fate of a convoy ambushed by a Shardbearer.

**Speak the Words** — An adventure set during the siege of Karanak. Heroes face a life-or-death situation as Odium's forces close in.

**Stormlight Scenarios** — Seven ready-to-drop adventure scenarios spanning tiers 1, 2, and 3. Range from the Herdazian countryside to the depths of Urithiru.

**Advanced Adversaries** — Expanded enemy profiles and threats drawn from across Roshar.

### What Each Book Is

**Mistborn Handbook** — The core rulebook for the Mistborn campaign setting. Covers the Plotweaver system adapted for Scadrial, character creation with Allomancy, Feruchemy, and Hemalurgy, and all the mechanics you need to run games in the Final Empire or the Era 2 political landscape.

**Mistborn World Guide** — Deep lore on Scadrial: the Final Empire, the Elendel Basin, factions, history, and adversary profiles. Essential companion to the Handbook for GMs building out their campaigns.

**Mistborn Legacy** — A full adventure set in the Mistborn world. Designed to drop into your campaign and introduce players to the threats and intrigue of Scadrial. Uniquely played across both Eras!

**Welcome to Scadrial** — A spoiler-conscious setting guide for players new to the Mistborn novels. Covers the world without giving away major plot beats. A great handout at session zero.

**Mistborn GM Screen** — A landscape GM screen with reference tables and art from the Mistborn setting. Keeps key rules at your fingertips during play.
