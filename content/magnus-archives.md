**The Magnus Archives Roleplaying Game** — The core rulebook. Built on the Cypher System, this brings the world of The Magnus Institute to the tabletop. Covers character creation, the Fear system, and everything you need to run your own horror investigations.

**The Magnus Archives Player's Guide** — A player-facing companion to the core rulebook. Great for getting your table up to speed without handing everyone the full GM book.

**The Magnus Archives Character Portfolio** — A collection of pre-generated characters and character sheets. Good for one-shots or getting new players into the game fast.

**The Magnus Archives Prop Set 1** — Printable props designed to bring the game's horror atmosphere to the table. Statements, documents, and handouts to pass to your players mid-session.

**The Magnus Archives XP Deck** — A physical card deck for tracking XP and advancement. Keeps the meta-progression tactile and visible at the table.

**Make Your Statement** — Ten ready-made investigations including nine new statements, with several adventures written by Jonathan Sims, co-creator of the podcast and voice of The Archivist. Suitable for investigators of all experience levels, from first-timers to Institute veterans.
